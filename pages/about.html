05 / ABOUT THE LAB

Creative craft.
Technical curiosity.

DNS ORIGIN LABS is an independent creative technology initiative focused on the space between visual craft and technical problem-solving.

THE IDEA

Build things that artists actually need.

The best production technology is grounded in the creative process it supports. This lab brings together digital compositing, CG lighting, color workflows and Python-based automation to create practical tools and share useful knowledge.

THE PRINCIPLES

Keep the work honest.

Artist-first thinking. Production-ready habits. Clear documentation. Reproducible results. Respect for project confidentiality and accurate credits. Learn in the open wherever it is appropriate to do so.
